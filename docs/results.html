🧭 Redocking validator

Docked the co-crystal ligand back into its own protein? Drop the ligand's reference PDB (crystal pose) here — the validator superposes pose vs crystal and scores how close you landed (redocking standard: RMSD ≤ 2 Å).

Redocking = docking a known crystal ligand back and checking you recover its pose. RMSD ≤ 2 Å is the community rule-of-thumb for "reproduced the crystal pose". If your dock lands > 2 Å away, check protonation, tautomers and box placement before blaming the score. This tool reports best-fit RMSD (optimal superposition of pose onto reference, Kabsch method) — more forgiving than the receptor-frame RMSD that docking benchmarks typically report, and insensitive to how the file was oriented. Atoms are matched by order, so it is only meaningful when pose and reference are the same molecule in the same atom order.
