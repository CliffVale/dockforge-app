Your first dock in four steps

1️⃣

Pick a kit

On the Docking Lab page choose 121D (DNA + netropsin, guided) or 1BNA (bring your own ligand). Files pre-load instantly.

2️⃣

Get the grid box

DockForge computes the box for you and pre-fills the Webina form — center and size included, with a screenshot walkthrough.

3️⃣

Run Webina

Docking runs in your browser tab (no server, no uploads). Typical kit run: a few minutes depending on your device.

4️⃣

Read the results

Drop the output on Result Viewer — ranked poses, best-fit RMSD vs reference, interactions and a printable notebook.
